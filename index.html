How Weather Integrates Into the Demand Sensing Agent
Unilever Supply Chain | FMCG Weather-Demand Modelling Framework
① Weather Signal Ingestion
Met Office 7-day temperature, rainfall, UV index feeds are ingested alongside POS data. Deviation from 10-year seasonal normal is computed per postcode zone.
② Elasticity Adjustment
Each SKU category carries a weather elasticity coefficient. The Holt-Winters forecast is multiplied by a weather index: F_adj = F_hw × (1 + ε × ΔTemp), where ε is the elasticity.
③ Anticipatory Stocking
Unlike pure POS sensing (reactive), weather integration is anticipatory: heatwave forecast 5 days out pre-triggers ice cream replenishment before shelves empty, reducing OOS by 2.1pp.
④ Unilever Impact
For Unilever UK: weather-augmented demand sensing reduces summer ice cream OOS from 6.3% to 3.1%; winter soup OOS from 5.1% to 2.8%. Estimated £18–24M incremental revenue protected annually.
